# 🏠 Housing Market Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Housing Market Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze property sales performance, regional market trends, pricing patterns, and real estate investment metrics.

The dashboard provides valuable insights into housing market behavior, helping stakeholders identify high-performing regions, evaluate property values, and understand key factors influencing real estate prices.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

* Analyze housing market trends and sales performance.
* Compare offer prices with actual purchase prices.
* Evaluate regional property market growth.
* Identify factors affecting property prices.
* Support data-driven real estate investment decisions.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ House Market Overview

* Year-over-Year Sales Growth Analysis
* Offer Price vs Purchase Price Comparison
* Median Sales Price Change by Region
* Units Sold Analysis
* Regional Market Performance Overview

### 2️⃣ Sales Performance

* Sales by Region
* Average Price per Square Meter (SQM)
* Key Influencers Analysis
* Offer-to-SQM Ratio by Sales Type
* Regional Revenue Comparison

### 3️⃣ Property & Investment Analysis

* Average Offer Price vs Purchase Price by House Type
* Inflation, Interest Rate, and Yield Analysis
* SQM vs SQM Price Comparison
* House Type Performance Analysis
* Interactive Filters for Region, City, and Sales Type

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights

✅ Zealand generated the highest housing sales volume.

✅ Regular sales achieved the highest Offer-to-SQM ratio.

✅ Apartment properties showed the highest average SQM pricing.

✅ Property prices varied significantly across regions and house types.

✅ Market performance was influenced by inflation, interest rates, and investment yields.
